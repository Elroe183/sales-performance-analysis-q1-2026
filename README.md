# Sales Performance Analysis — Q1 2026

Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance during the first quarter of 2026 using Microsoft Excel. The goal is to identify revenue trends, compare product performance, evaluate profitability, and provide actionable business recommendations.

Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and formulas
- Data visualization and dashboard design
- Business analysis

Key Performance Indicators

Metric| Result
Total Revenue| 23,730
Total Profit| 7,771
Profit Margin| 32.75%
Top Product by Revenue| Laptop

Key Findings

1. Monthly Revenue Trend: Revenue decreased from 8,760 in January to 5,390 in February, a decline of 38.47%. It then increased to 9,580 in March, a recovery of 77.74% compared with February.
2. Product Performance: Laptop generated 8,550, or 36.03% of total revenue, making it the leading product by revenue.
3. Revenue Concentration: Laptop and Phone together generated approximately 70.16% of total revenue.
4. Profitability: Total profit was 7,771, with an overall profit margin of approximately 32.75%.

Business Recommendations

- Investigate the February revenue decline by reviewing sales quantities, customer demand, inventory availability, and promotions.
- Monitor Laptop and Phone demand to help manage inventory effectively.
- Review product-level profit margins before making decisions about increasing stock.
- Continue monitoring monthly revenue and profitability using the dashboard.

Deliverables

- Excel sales analysis workbook
- Sales performance dashboard
- Revenue trend and product performance charts
- Business findings and recommendations

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Excel can be used to analyze business data, identify performance trends, and support data-driven decisions.
